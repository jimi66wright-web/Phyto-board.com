Overview

The Phyto-Fortress is the first building-scale demonstration of the Phyto-board® system: a timber skeleton carries the structural load, a reactive hemp-hurd geopolymer infill fills and locks the envelope together, and Phyto-board panels form the continuous interior and exterior skins. It ships as a shell — framed openings, skid base, and core envelope complete — with doors, glazing, HVAC, and interior fit-out left to the installer.

How it bonds

The reactive hemp-hurd geopolymer isn't a filler poured into a cavity — it's a chemical bonding system. The underlying chemistry class starts from five inputs: hemp hurd, crystalline silica, silanes, lime, and metakaolin.

StepReaction
1. Surface preparationHydroxyl (–OH) groups on hemp hurd and silanol (Si–OH) groups on silica become available for bonding
2. Silane couplingSilanes hydrolyze and bridge — one end bonds to hemp hurd, the other to silica
3. Lime activationLime dissolves, releasing Ca²⁺ and OH⁻, raising pH to activate silica and metakaolin surfaces
4. Pozzolanic reactionMetakaolin dissolves in the high-pH environment; Si and Al species react with Ca²⁺ to form C-S-H and C-A-H hydrates
5. Matrix formationHydrate gels grow and encapsulate the hemp hurd and silica, filling pores into a dense, low-porosity matrix

Phyto-board's production formulation extends this baseline chemistry with additional coupling agents and activation chemistry, produced under in-process quality assurance controls, to reliably yield the HD, MD, and LT density grades used across the panel and Phyto-Fortress product lines.

Assembly sequence

  1. Set the skid foundation
    Position the 8' × 16' timber skid with steel runners on a level, drained base (concrete or naturally firm foundation). Confirm sill gasket / moisture barrier is in place.
  2. Erect the timber skeleton
    Assemble the offset 2×4 wall, roof, and floor framing per the numbered kit sequence. This frame carries the full structural load — panels and infill do not.
  3. Install Phyto-board panels
    Place HD exterior panels first, then MD interior panels, on the framed skeleton — leaving the continuous cavity between them for infill.
  4. Place and secure corner molds
    Fit the reusable radius-corner molds (small or large radius, per spec) at each vertical corner and transition before pouring infill.
  5. Mix and place geopolymer infill
    Mix hemp hurd, silica, silanes, lime, and metakaolin with locally sourced water per the kit's mix ratios. Place into the wall/roof/floor cavities and compact.
  6. Cure
    Allow the reactive infill to cure per the assembly guide's schedule before removing molds or loading the structure.
  7. Remove molds, finish corners
    Strip corner molds once cured. Corner is now complete and part of the continuous structural envelope.
  8. Install openings and utilities
    Fit doors, windows, and any specified vapor/weather components. Route service penetrations through sealed, compatible fittings.

Kit tiers

Basic

Economical timber-frame shell with Phyto-board envelope and simple skid connection.

Intermediate

Adds hemp scrim reinforcement, hurricane straps, stronger opening blocking, and reinforced anchor connections.

Tier One

Adds a protected internal steel storm core with reinforced opening frame, roof tie rail, diagonal tension strap, and steel-reinforced sill connection.
