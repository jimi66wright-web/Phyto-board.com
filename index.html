Chapter 0 · The Paradigm

We don't sell panels.
We create one structure.

Most buildings fail as assemblies — five products, five trades, five insurance events.

Conventional envelopes are a sandwich: sheathing, insulation, wraps, and finishes stacked by different crews. Phyto-Board is the opposite premise — one reactive chemistry, graded by density, packed into a binder-coated timber skeleton so floor, wall, and ceiling — one chemistry, one family of hemp-based products. OSB4 is how the market keeps score; we take share by retiring the sandwich, not by joining the wall.
